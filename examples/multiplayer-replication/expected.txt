Alice and bob joined the server.

The server spawns a player for each, made by the spawn function everywhere:
  alice: spawned alice_player
  alice: spawned bob_player
  bob: spawned alice_player
  bob: spawned bob_player
  on bob's world, alice_player answers to alice and is at 100, 100

Alice moves her player; her synchronizer sends it to the others:
  the server sees her at 140, 90, bob at 140, 90
  bob moves alice's player on his own world, and the next sync puts it back:
  bob sees her at 140, 90

The server fires a bullet: placed under the arena, it spawns everywhere:
  alice: spawned bullet1
  bob: spawned bullet1
  it starts at 100, 200 on alice's world
  it flies on the server, and alice sees it at 200, 200
  it hits and turns red; the change reaches bob reliably

A treasure only alice can see:
  alice: spawned treasure
  bob has it: 0

The bullet is gone on the server, so it despawns everywhere:
  alice: despawned bullet1
  bob: despawned bullet1

Bob leaves; the server destroys his player, which despawns on alice's:
  alice: despawned bob_player
