Before: 3D frames had no ambient occlusion. Corners, creases and the ground under objects were lit as brightly as open surfaces, so scenes looked flat, and Godot's `Environment` SSAO fields were reported and dropped on import.

After: a `WorldEnvironment`'s `ssao_enabled` darkens what light can't easily reach, as Godot 4.6's Forward+ renderer does with Intel's ASSAO, with Godot's radius, intensity, power, detail, horizon, sharpness and light affect fields. project.json's `rendering.environment.ssao` picks Godot's quality (very low to ultra), half size, blur passes, adaptive target and fade distances. Vulkan, Metal, Direct3D 12, WebGPU and the software device (so `frame.capture` too) draw it, and Godot projects bring the fields and settings over.

How:
- **Passes.** A new prepass draws the opaque meshes' depth and view-space normals (`mesh_normal.hlsl`, the mesh shader built with `NORMAL_ROUGHNESS`). `ssao.hlsl` holds Godot's SSAO compute shaders as fragment passes: the depth made linear and deinterleaved into four slices at up to four levels (`ss_effects_downsample.glsl`), the gather (`ssao.glsl`, with ultra's base pass, importance map and adaptive gather of up to 32 samples), the edge-aware blur (`ssao_blur.glsl`) and the interleave back to the frame's size (`ssao_interleave.glsl`). `ssao_plan` lists the passes for a quality and size, `ssao_uniforms` fills Godot's push constants, and `run_ssao_pass` is the CPU twin. The passes load texels and filter in code (WebGPU filters no floats), so every device draws the same values.
- **Applying it.** `mesh.hlsl` and its CPU twin read the result as Godot's scene shader does: it multiplies ambient light, which dims reflections through Godot's specular occlusion, and `ssao_light_affect` times `ssao_ao_channel_affect` of it darkens direct light. Transparent meshes get none, and `gl_compatibility` has no SSAO, as in Godot.
- **Devices.** The SDL GPU and WebGPU devices draw the prepass and the passes into targets kept between frames and sized by the plan, and bind the result to the mesh pass (white when SSAO is off). The software device draws the same prepass on the CPU. The shader header for `ssao.hlsl` is written in pieces, since its code passes the 64 KB string literal limit of Clang and MSVC.
- **Fields.** `WorldEnvironment` gains Godot's nine `ssao_*` fields and defaults; `scene3d::environment` copies them into `render::Ssao3D`. `Renderer::prepare` takes `render::SsaoSettings`, which games, sessions and `frame.capture` read from project.json.
- **Import.** An `Environment`'s SSAO fields now convert with the rest, and `rendering/environment/ssao/*` project settings become `rendering.environment.ssao`.
- **Tests.** `test_ssao.cpp`: plans for every quality and size, uniforms as Godot's push constants, linear depth at known distances (perspective and orthogonal), every quality darkening a corner and the wall around a square on every Forward+ device while the open floor keeps its light, no occlusion at intensity 0, transparent meshes left alone, light affect, Compatibility unchanged, and the GPU matching the software device within 2 levels across qualities, sizes, blur and adaptive settings. `test_scene3d.cpp`: fields and settings reaching the frame, a captured frame darkening a wall around a slab, and project.json's setting checked and read. `test_import_godot.cpp`: the fields and project settings.
- **Example.** The new `examples/ssao-3d` darkens a room's corners and the floor and wall around a crate, makes the occlusion stronger and wider with `component.set`, darkens direct light too, then turns it off.
- **Docs.** scene-format.md's `WorldEnvironment` fields and an Ambient occlusion section, project-format.md's new SSAO section, godot-import.md, AGENTS.md and the roadmap.
