Before: shiny floors and metal in 3D rooms reflected the sky even indoors, and only what was on screen through SSR; Godot's and Unity's reflection probes were reported and dropped on import.

After: a `ReflectionProbe` is a box whose meshes reflect a cube drawn from inside it, as Godot 4.6's Forward+ `ReflectionProbe`: a chrome ball shows the wall behind the camera, and a polished floor reflects the room rather than the sky. It has Godot's fields: update mode, intensity, blend distance, max distance, size, origin offset, box projection, interior, shadows, cull and reflection masks, and the three ambient modes. Probes share one atlas of cubes sized by project.json's `rendering.reflections.reflection_atlas`, as Godot's project setting. Vulkan, Metal, Direct3D 12, WebGPU and the software device (so `frame.capture` too) draw them, and Godot and Unity scenes bring their probes over.

How:
- **Captures.** `scene3d::Renderer::prepare` gives each visible probe a cube of the atlas (a texture made with `TextureDesc::cubes`) and, when the probe first shows or its place, box, offset, distance, layers, interior or shadows change (or every frame with `"always"`), draws its six faces in frames of their own on the device, as Godot draws a probe's sides. Each face's camera (`probe_face_view`, `probe_face_projection`) looks along an axis from `origin_offset` as far as the box reaches, or `max_distance`; a capture frame (`render::ProbeCapture`) draws the world linear and unprocessed, with no post-processing, SSAO, SSR or probes, and `interior` leaves out the sky, fog and ambient light. After the sixth face the cube's levels are filtered from it as the sky's radiance is (`radiance.hlsl`).
- **Reflecting.** `mesh.hlsl`'s Forward+ variant runs Godot's `reflection_process` (`scene_forward_lights_inc.glsl`) for each probe whose box holds the pixel and whose `reflection_mask` takes the mesh: box projection, the level for the pixel's roughness, the blend toward the box's sides and the ambient modes, smallest probes first and larger ones filling what is left, with the sky's light taking the rest. `render::detail::reflect_probes` (`reflection.cpp`) is its CPU twin, which the software device draws with. A probe's light is scaled by the camera's exposure over the one it was drawn with, as Godot's exposure normalization.
- **Devices.** The SDL GPU and WebGPU devices keep the atlas as a cube array, draw captures into its faces and filter its levels, and bind it to the mesh pass with the probes' uniforms; the software device keeps its cubes as images. `check_reflections` checks a frame's probes and capture the same way on every device. The OpenGL devices (`gl_compatibility`) keep no atlas and draw no probes yet, so Compatibility frames are unchanged.
- **Settings.** `render::ReflectionAtlasSettings` reads `rendering.reflections.reflection_atlas` (`reflection_size` 32 to 2048, and `reflection_count` 1 to 4096 as Godot's, of which a frame draws at most 42: 42 cubes of six faces are the 256 texture layers every GPU and WebGPU take); games, sessions and `frame.capture` pass it to the renderer, and `project validate` reports a bad size or count.
- **Import.** Godot's `ReflectionProbe` nodes become `ReflectionProbe` components with their fields, and the `reflection_atlas` project settings come over. Unity's reflection probes convert with their box, intensity, blend distance, box projection, far clip, shadows and culling mask, and no ambient light of their own, as Unity's give none; custom cubemaps, background colors, script refreshes and turned or scaled probes are reported.
- **Tests.** `test_reflection.cpp`: the atlas settings, each face's camera and reach, `reflect_probes` against Godot's rules (box projection, order, masks, blending, ambient modes), a room reflected in its floor on every Forward+ device, the GPUs drawing captures and probes as the software device does, and devices refusing bad probes and captures. `test_scene3d.cpp`: probes reaching the frame largest first with cubes in one atlas, and a captured frame reflecting a glowing cube in a polished floor. `test_import_godot.cpp` and `test_import_unity.cpp`: the converted probes and settings.
- **Example.** The new `examples/reflection-probes-3d` reflects a room in a chrome ball and a polished floor, turns on box projection, roughens the floor and hides the probe. `examples/import-unity-3d` now converts its courtyard's probe.
