Before: every 3D frame was in focus from the camera to the horizon. Godot's `CameraAttributesPractical` depth of field fields were reported and dropped on import, and there was no way to blur a background behind a character or rack focus in a cutscene.

After: a camera's `CameraAttributesPractical` (or the `WorldEnvironment`'s) blurs what is past `dof_blur_far_distance` and nearer than `dof_blur_near_distance`, as Godot 4.6's Forward+ renderer does. project.json's `rendering.camera.depth_of_field` picks Godot's bokeh shape (box, hexagon or circle), quality and jitter. Vulkan, Metal, Direct3D 12, WebGPU and the software device (so `frame.capture` too) draw it, and Godot projects bring the fields and settings over.

How:
- **Passes.** `forward_post.hlsl` gains Godot's `bokeh_dof.glsl` passes, run before glow and auto exposure: each pixel's blur size from the depth buffer (`get_blur_size`, physical when a transition is negative), the box or hexagon blur in two passes (half size at `very_low` and `low`) or the circle's golden-angle spiral at half size, and the composite that mixes the blur in by each pixel's size. `dof_plan` lists the steps for a shape and quality, `dof_uniforms` fills Godot's push constants, and `run_dof_size_pass`, `run_dof_blur_pass` and `run_dof_composite_pass` are the CPU twins. Frames under 8x8 aren't blurred, as Godot's `can_use_effects`. Jitter follows Godot's `hash12n`, seeded by game time instead of a random number so frames repeat.
- **Devices.** The SDL GPU and WebGPU devices keep the depth buffer (stored, and sampled as an unfilterable texture) when depth of field is on, and five more targets for the steps. The software device returns its depth from `draw_meshes`. The world the post passes read is the depth of field's output, so luminance, glow and the tonemap see the blurred frame.
- **Fields.** `CameraAttributesPractical` gains Godot's seven `dof_blur_*` fields and defaults; `scene3d::environment` copies them into `render::PostProcess`. `Renderer::prepare` takes `render::DepthOfFieldSettings`, which games, sessions and `frame.capture` read from project.json.
- **Import.** A `CameraAttributesPractical`'s depth of field fields now convert with the rest, and `rendering/camera/depth_of_field/*` project settings become `rendering.camera.depth_of_field`.
- **Tests.** `test_forward_post.cpp`: plans for every shape and quality, uniforms as Godot's push constants, blur sizes at known distances (smooth and physical), what each shape and quality blurs on every Forward+ device, and the GPU matching the software device across shapes, qualities, physical transitions, jitter, and auto exposure with glow. `test_scene3d.cpp`: fields into the post-process settings, jitter seeds from game time, a captured frame blurring past the far distance, and project.json's setting checked and read. `test_import_godot.cpp`: the fields and project settings.
- **Example.** The new `examples/depth-of-field-3d` focuses on a statue between a near lantern and a far fence, racks focus to the fence with `component.set`, blurs harder, then turns blur off.
- **Docs.** scene-format.md's `CameraAttributesPractical` fields and how the blur works, project-format.md's new Depth of field section, godot-import.md, AGENTS.md and the roadmap.
