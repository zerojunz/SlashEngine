Before: light in 3D scenes was only ever drawn live, so walls never lit the floor beside them and rooms got the same flat ambient light from the sky in every corner; Godot's `LightmapGI` nodes were reported and dropped on import.

After: a `LightmapGI` bakes the light of the static meshes under its parent, as Godot 4.6's `LightmapGI`: `lightmap.bake` traces direct light with soft shadows, bounces between surfaces and the environment's light into a `.lightmap.json` file and the `.hdr` file of its texels, and lightmapped meshes draw with it, so a sunlit wall warms the floor next to it and the floor cools when the wall is painted blue and baked again. It has Godot's fields (quality, bounces, bounce energy, texture for bounces, denoiser, bias, texel scale, supersampling, environment mode and camera attributes), meshes have Godot's `gi_mode` and `gi_lightmap_texel_scale`, and lights Godot's `light_bake_mode` (disabled, dynamic or static), indirect energy, size and shadow blur. Vulkan, Metal, Direct3D 12, WebGPU, both OpenGL devices and the software device (so `frame.capture` too) draw lightmaps the same way.

How:
- **Unwrapping.** `scene3d::unwrap_lightmap` cuts each mesh into charts with xatlas, as Godot's "Unwrap UV2 for Lightmap/AO" does, at Godot's 0.2 units a texel. `src/xatlas_build.cpp` builds xatlas and stb_rect_pack with the engine's own sines, cosines and arc functions and a stable sort in place of their `qsort`, on one thread, so charts and atlases come out the same on every platform. Unwraps are stored in the lightmap by a hash of the mesh's positions, normals and triangles (`lightmap_mesh_hash`), so a mesh that changes is baked again rather than drawn with stale UVs.
- **Baking.** `bake_lightmap` (`src/lightmap.cpp`) gathers a LightmapGI's static `MeshInstance3D`s (primitive meshes and model parts; skinned parts are left out with a problem), its baking lights and the environment its `environment_mode` names. `src/lightmapper.cpp` follows Godot's `LightmapperRD` and its `lm_compute.glsl` pass by pass on the CPU: the unwraps packed into slices, albedo and emission baked into UV2 space, direct light with soft shadows by `light_size` and `shadow_blur`, bounces (from the lightmap's own direct light with `use_texture_for_bounces`), the environment's light, Godot's JNLM denoiser, dilation and seam blending. Texels are traced on the job pool and are the same for any thread count. "static" lights bake their direct light too and aren't lit live on lightmapped meshes; "dynamic" ones bake only their bounces, as Godot's.
- **Files.** A `.lightmap.json` file (`$cli schema lightmap`) is Godot's `LightmapGIData`: the texels' `.hdr` file (Radiance RGBE, `render::encode_hdr`/`decode_hdr`), the slices' size, the exposure it was baked with, the unwraps by hash (arrays as base64 of little-endian values) and each mesh's slice and rectangle. `lightmap.bake` writes both files with undo, takes `dry_run`, sets `light_data`, and says `unchanged` when nothing moved. `scene validate`, `project validate` and exports check LightmapGIs (`check_lightmaps`) and the files, and exports stage them.
- **Drawing.** `scene3d::Renderer` loads each LightmapGI's files, gives each lightmapped mesh its `uv2` from the unwrap stored for its hash, and draws it with `Material3D::lightmap` (`render::LightmapShading`, texture slot 10): `mesh.hlsl`'s `lightmap_light` samples the slice bicubically, as Godot's, in place of the environment's ambient light and reflection probes' ambient light, and `render::detail::lightmap_light` is its CPU twin. `render::Vertex3D` gains `uv2`, which the shadow and mesh shaders carry (the GLSL ES varyings are matched by name), and the shaders' SPIR-V, MSL, GLSL and WGSL are regenerated.
- **Import.** Godot's `LightmapGI` nodes convert with their fields and camera attributes; their baked `light_data` is in Godot's own formats, so it is reported and `lightmap.bake` makes it again.
- **Tests.** `test_lightmap.cpp`: half floats and HDR files, unwraps the same every time, `.lightmap.json` validation, a LightmapGI baking light, shadows and bounces as Godot's, bakes the same on any thread count, lightmapped meshes drawing their baked light in place of baked lights, and every device drawing lightmaps as the software device does. `test_command.cpp`: `lightmap.bake` writing, dry runs, `unchanged` and undo. `test_import_godot.cpp`: the converted LightmapGI.
- **Example.** The new `examples/lightmaps-3d` captures a courtyard live, bakes it, paints the wall blue, bakes again and captures the cooler floor. `import-godot-character` and `import-godot-skeleton` print the new fields.
- **Dependencies.** xatlas (MIT) is new in `cmake/dependencies.json` and THIRD_PARTY.md; stb's row adds `stb_rect_pack.h`, and the JNLM denoiser Godot's lightmapper uses (MIT) is credited.
