Before: a baked `LightmapGI` lit only the static meshes it baked; a mesh that moves (a character, a ball, a door) still took the scene's flat ambient light, so it looked pasted on in a room whose walls and floor glowed with bounced light. Godot's `LightmapProbe` nodes and a LightmapGI's `interior` and `generate_probes_subdiv` were dropped on import.

After: a bake also makes light probes, as Godot 4.6's `LightmapGI` does, and a `MeshInstance3D` whose `gi_mode` is `"dynamic"` takes its ambient light from them, eased as it moves: a ball rolling from the sunny floor into a red-lit corner picks up the red bounce. Probes go on an octree over the static meshes (`generate_probes_subdiv`, from `"disabled"` to `"32"`) and at `LightmapProbe` entities, a LightmapGI can be `interior`, and `lightmap.bake` reports how many probes it baked. Vulkan, Metal, Direct3D 12, WebGPU, both OpenGL devices and the software device (so `frame.capture` too) draw the probes' light the same way.

How:
- **Placement.** `scene3d::place_lightmap_probes` follows `LightmapGI::bake`: bounds around the static meshes and the LightmapProbes, the bounds' corners, then the corners of the octree cells the meshes' triangles touch (`_plot_triangle_into_octree`, `_gen_new_positions_from_octree`), leaving out those within half a cell of a LightmapProbe.
- **Baking.** The lightmapper traces each probe after the bounces, as `LightmapperRD`'s probe pass does (64, 256, 512 or 2048 rays by `quality`, on the job pool), into nine spherical harmonics coefficients in Godot's order, turned into the LightmapGI's space.
- **Tetrahedra.** `scene3d::tetrahedralize` ports Godot's `Delaunay3D`: Bowyer-Watson over points scaled into a unit cube and quantized, with its acceleration grid. One difference: Godot tests whether a point is inside a circumsphere in 128-bit fixed point with a tolerance, which lets tetrahedra overlap when points lie in flat layers or on a grid, as the probes over a bare floor do (their tetrahedra came to twice the hull's volume, and the BSP tree then ran for minutes). Here the test is exact, with Jonathan Shewchuk's expansion arithmetic (public domain): the probes' coordinates are all multiples of 2^-16, so the differences and 2x2 minors are exact doubles and the rest is summed exactly when a quick estimate can't tell the sign. The circumspheres only pick the grid cells, in doubles grown by their rounding error, and the grid drops dead simplices lazily instead of keeping a linked list per cell. A floor of 2,178 probes now joins in under a second. `lightmap_bsp_tree` ports `LightmapGI::_compute_bsp_tree` (faces scored on the job pool, the same tree for any thread count), and `tap_lightmap_probes` ports `LightStorage::lightmap_tap_sh_light`.
- **Capture.** `lightmap_capture` ports `_update_instance_lightmap_captures`: each LightmapGI whose bounds hold the mesh's middle is tapped, its light turned into the world with `rotate_sh` and scaled by the camera's exposure over the baked one, blended by how far inside it the mesh is, interior LightmapGIs standing in for the rest. `rotate_sh` is John Hable's code, as Godot's `Basis::rotate_sh`, with one difference: Hable's code expects the odd orders' signs negated (the Condon-Shortley phase), which Godot's lightmapper doesn't use, so Godot turns a rotated LightmapGI's light mirrored in z; here the signs are flipped around it, and a test checks that turned light looked at along a turned direction is the same as before. `scene3d::Renderer` eases each dynamic mesh's capture at Godot's update speed of 15 a second and passes it as `Material3D::capture`.
- **Drawing.** `mesh.hlsl`'s `capture_light` evaluates the coefficients at each pixel's normal, as Godot's `INSTANCE_FLAGS_USE_LIGHTMAP_CAPTURE` does, in place of the scene's and reflection probes' ambient light; `render::detail::capture_light` is its CPU twin. The surface uniforms grow by 144 bytes, and the shaders' SPIR-V, MSL, GLSL and WGSL are regenerated.
- **Files.** `.lightmap.json` files gain `probes`: bounds, interior and, as base64, points, light, tetrahedra and BSP nodes, all checked against each other by `validate_lightmap`.
- **Import.** Godot's `LightmapProbe` nodes convert, and a LightmapGI keeps `interior` and `generate_probes_subdiv`.
- **Tests.** `test_lightmap.cpp`: probe placement, Delaunay tetrahedra filling their hull with empty circumspheres, the BSP tree and tap blending linear light back exactly, `rotate_sh` and blending between LightmapGIs, `.lightmap.json` probes and their errors, a bake's probes, moving meshes taking and easing the probes' light, and every device drawing it as the software device does. `test_import_godot.cpp`: the converted LightmapProbe and fields.
- **Example.** `examples/lightmaps-3d` adds a dynamic ball that takes the probes' light once the courtyard is baked.
- **Docs.** scene-format.md gains a Light probes section, and commands.md, godot-import.md, AGENTS.md and the roadmap follow.
